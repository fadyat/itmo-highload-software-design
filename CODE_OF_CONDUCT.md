# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Sustained disruption of community events, projects, or discussions.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community. Examples of representing the project include using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement and Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue in this repository and adding the label `conduct`, or by contacting the repository maintainers directly (see `docs/design.md` for maintainer contacts). If you prefer to report privately, please contact a maintainer via the contact information listed in `docs/design.md`.

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality to the extent possible.

### How we respond

When a report is received, the maintainers will:

1. Acknowledge receipt of the report within a reasonable timeframe.
2. Evaluate and investigate the report promptly and fairly.
3. Determine appropriate actions consistent with the severity and frequency of the behavior, which may include:
   - Private warnings to the reporter and/or the respondent.
   - Temporary or permanent removal of commenting or contribution privileges.
   - Requesting remediation steps (e.g., an apology, code of conduct training).
   - Escalation to hosting platform or other authorities when applicable.

Decisions may be made by one or more maintainers and, where appropriate, a small, confidential group of community representatives.

## Enforcement Guidelines

To ensure consistency, the maintainers aim to apply these guidelines reasonably and fairly. Factors considered when deciding a response include, but are not limited to:

- The severity of the behavior.
- Whether it was a single incident or a repeated pattern.
- Any mitigating factors or evidence of remediation.
- The safety and well-being of the reporting party and the broader community.

## Appeals

If you believe a decision was unjust, you may request an appeal by contacting the maintainers (contacts in `docs/design.md`) and providing relevant context and any additional information. Appeals will be reviewed by a different maintainer or an appointed neutral person where possible.
